# Launch plan — 14 days to first sale

> **Superseded on 2026-09-05.** Pipeline is released free rather than sold, so the
> storefront and pricing steps no longer apply. The promotion channel list and the
> UE5 verification step are still valid for a free release.

Goal: first money fast. Blender Market as the storefront. Marketing by posting,
never on camera.

---

## Days 1–2 · Verify and fix

- [ ] Run `store/UE5-CHECK.md` on the machine with Unreal
- [ ] Report the four results back; fix anything that fails
- [ ] Remove the honesty gate from the listing once all four pass

**Nothing else starts until this is done.** Every remaining task assumes the
add-on genuinely works in Unreal.

---

## Days 2–4 · Make the assets

- [ ] Five screenshots, briefed shot by shot in `store/LISTING.md`
- [ ] The 60-second demo, scripted beat by beat with timecodes in the same file
- [ ] Export a 15-second silent **GIF or MP4** of just the export happening —
      this is the single most reused asset. Every forum and social post leads
      with it, so it needs to read at thumbnail size with no sound and no context.

Record at 1920×1080, Blender's default dark theme, and hide your bookmarks bar and
desktop clutter. Presentation is a large part of what separates a $19 add-on from a
free script in a buyer's mind.

---

## Days 3–5 · Storefront, in parallel

**Blender Market** is the primary. Creators apply and are reviewed before they can
list, so start the application while you are still editing video — the wait is dead
time otherwise. Check their current submission requirements directly; they change.

**Set up Gumroad or Payhip the same week as a backup.** If Blender Market's review
is slow or says no, you still have somewhere to sell on launch day, and the direct
link is useful in posts regardless. Both act as merchant of record, which keeps
EU/US sales tax off your plate from Malaysia.

- [ ] Apply to Blender Market
- [ ] Set up the backup store and upload the same listing
- [ ] Confirm payout works to a Malaysian account before launch, not after

---

## Days 5–10 · Post it

Lead with the video every time. Never open with "please buy my add-on" — open with
the problem, show the fix, mention the product last.

**Highest value first:**

- [ ] **Blender Artists forum** → the released-add-ons section. This is where
      Blender add-ons are actually discovered. Write a real post: the problem, the
      video, what it does, what it does not do, the price.
- [ ] **BlenderNation** — they cover new add-ons and accept tips. One email with
      the video and a paragraph. A single mention here outperforms weeks of
      social posting.
- [ ] **r/unrealengine** — framed as a Blender-to-Unreal workflow tool, which is
      the audience's actual pain
- [ ] **r/blender** — read their self-promotion rules first, they are enforced
- [ ] **Unreal Slackers Discord** — the tools and plugins channel
- [ ] **X/Twitter** — the `#b3d` tag is genuinely active, plus `#UE5`
- [ ] **LinkedIn** — your professional network and Todak Academy's reach
- [ ] **Behance** — post it as a project alongside your existing twelve

**One post per day, not all at once.** Spreading them keeps you visible for a week
instead of one afternoon, and gives you time to answer replies — answering fast is
most of what converts a viewer into a buyer.

---

## Days 10–14 · Respond and patch

- [ ] Answer every comment and question within a day
- [ ] Ship a 1.0.1 for anything real that surfaces — a fast fix builds more trust
      than a flawless launch
- [ ] Ask your first few buyers for a review. Reviews carry the listing afterwards

---

## What to actually expect

Be realistic so you can judge the result honestly rather than by feel.

A first product, from an unknown seller, with a good demo video, priced at $19,
typically sees **single digits to a few dozen sales in month one**. At 20 sales
after Blender Market's cut that is roughly **$260**.

That is not life-changing money, and it is not supposed to be. What it buys is:

- Proof the whole loop works — build, list, sell, get paid into a Malaysian account
- A public product with your name on it, which makes product #2 easier to sell
- Real buyer feedback, which is worth more than the revenue at this stage

The compounding happens across products. One add-on is a trickle; an add-on plus
two asset packs plus a course, all cross-linking to each other, is a business. The
add-on exists to prove the pipeline works before you spend weeks modelling.
